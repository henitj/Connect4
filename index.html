How to Play

Objective

Connect 4 of your pieces in a row - horizontally, vertically, or diagonally - to win!

How to Play

  • Tap a column to drop your piece
  • Pieces fall to the lowest empty spot
  • Red always goes first
  • Take turns with your opponent

Tips

  • Control the center column
  • Create multiple winning threats
  • Block your opponent's lines
  • Plan several moves ahead
